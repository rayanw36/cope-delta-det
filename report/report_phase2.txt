2. PHASE A: REAL CODEC MVs VIA H.264 PROXY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem: Block-matching estimates ≠ true codec motion vectors. The block
matching approach in extract_features.py uses SAD-based search which is
qualitatively different from the actual motion estimation the codec performs.

Discovery: FFmpeg's HEVC decoder does NOT populate MOTION_VECTORS side_data.
This is a known limitation — only the H.264 decoder supports export_mvs.
Confirmed by testing both HEVC (0 MV frames after 100+ decoded) and H.264
(3321 MVs found on frame 1).

Solution: H.264 proxy transcoding pipeline:
  1. Decode HEVC source → raw frames
  2. Encode to H.264 via PyAV (libx264, preset=ultrafast, g=16, bf=0)
  3. Decode H.264 with flags2=+export_mvs
  4. Extract MVs from side_data['MOTION_VECTORS'] per frame
  5. Convert sparse block-level MVs to dense (45, 80, 2) grid

New file: data/extract_features_pyav.py

Performance comparison (per video, ~1217 frames):
  Block-matching (GPU):  ~20s per video, 61 fps aggregate (3 workers)
  H.264 proxy (PyAV):   ~14s per video, 87 fps single

MV quality comparison (3 sample videos):
  +------------------+----------+----------+
  | Metric           | Block-BM | PyAV MV  |
  +------------------+----------+----------+
  | Avg MV magnitude |    4.23  |    1.95  |
  | Coverage (%)     |    53.3  |    38.5  |
  | Direction cosine |    ---   |    0.51  |
  +------------------+----------+----------+

Key differences:
  - PyAV MVs are sparser and smaller magnitude (codec efficiently skips
    static blocks, while block-matching always finds "some" match)
  - Direction cosine similarity 0.35-0.62 shows reasonable correlation
  - PyAV MVs better reflect true motion (skip blocks get zero, not noise)

Dataset.py updated: features_subdir parameter (default='features') allows
switching between 'features' (block-matched) and 'features_pyav' (PyAV).

Status: PyAV extraction COMPLETE — all 2000 videos processed.

PyAV vs Block-Matched MV comparison (both trained 30 epochs):
  +----------------------------------+----------+--------------+
  | CoPE MV Source                   | mAP@50   | mAP@[.5:.95] |
  +----------------------------------+----------+--------------+
  | Block-Matched (GPU SAD)          |  18.90%  |     8.15%    |
  | PyAV H.264 Proxy (real codec)    |  12.30%  |     5.07%    |
  +----------------------------------+----------+--------------+

Conclusion: Block-matched MVs OUTPERFORM H.264 proxy MVs by 6.6pp.
Reason: H.264 proxy MVs come from a different codec than the HEVC source.
The block-matching approach directly measures motion between the actual
decoded frames, producing MVs that better align with the detection task.
H.264 proxy MVs are sparser (38% vs 53% coverage) due to aggressive
skip-mode decisions with ultrafast preset.

Recommendation: Keep block-matched MVs as the primary MV source.
The PyAV approach would only help if we could extract HEVC MVs directly
(requires FFmpeg HEVC decoder changes or a custom HEVC parser).


3. PHASE B: YOLO FULL mAP DEBUGGING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem: YOLO Full baseline achieved only 17.07% mAP@50, far below the
expected 30-50% for YOLOv8m on driving scenes.

Root cause found: BDD100K annotation category name mismatch!

  prepare_bdd100k.py used class names:     BDD100K JSONs use:
    'pedestrian'                             'person'
    'bicycle'                                'bike'
    'motorcycle'                             'motor'

This caused 3 classes to be silently dropped from all ground truth:
  - 'person' (144 boxes per 100 videos) → ZERO in GT
  - 'bike' (6 boxes per 100 videos) → ZERO in GT
  - 'motor' (2 boxes per 100 videos) → ZERO in GT

Fix: Created ANNOTATION_CATEGORY_MAP with all known name variants:
  person→0, pedestrian→0, bike→7, bicycle→7, motor→6, motorcycle→6

Additional findings from diagnostic analysis:
  - 62.2% of GT boxes are small (<32px) — inherently hard for YOLO
  - Traffic sign: 317 GT boxes with near-zero YOLO detections (COCO "stop
    sign" ≠ BDD "traffic sign" which includes all types)
  - BDD "rider" (class 1) has no COCO mapping — detected as "person"
  - I-frame vs propagated GT: mAP drops from 22.8% to 21.0% due to MV drift

Results BEFORE fix:
  +---------------+----------+---------------+
  | Method        | mAP@0.50 | mAP@[.5:.95]  |
  +---------------+----------+---------------+
  | YOLO Full     |  17.07%  |     9.90%     |
  | Copy-Paste    |  14.21%  |     8.30%     |
  | CoPE-Delta-Det|  14.24%  |     8.62%     |
  +---------------+----------+---------------+

Results AFTER fix:
  +---------------+----------+---------------+----------------+-----------------+
  | Method        | mAP@0.50 | mAP@[.5:.95]  | Decode Budget  | Latency per GOP |
  +---------------+----------+---------------+----------------+-----------------+
  | YOLO Full     |  21.01%  |    10.58%     |   100.0%       |   230.0 ms      |
  | Copy-Paste    |  13.66%  |     ---       |     6.2%       |    15.5 ms      |
  | CoPE-Delta-Det|  17.10%  |     ---       |     6.2%       |    93.3 ms      |
  +---------------+----------+---------------+----------------+-----------------+

Improvement: YOLO Full +3.94pp, CoPE +2.86pp (both from corrected GT).
CoPE now shows clear advantage over Copy-Paste (+3.44pp).

Per-class AP@50 (YOLO Full, annotated frame only, 100 GOPs):
  pedestrian:      41.42%  (was 0% — no GT before fix!)
  car:             46.42%
  truck:           35.13%
  motorcycle:      50.50%
  bus:             40.74%
  bicycle:         33.66%
  traffic light:   17.81%  (many small objects)
  traffic sign:     0.00%  (COCO stop sign ≠ general traffic sign)
  rider:            0.00%  (no COCO mapping)
  train:            0.00%  (rare class, 1 instance in 100 videos)

Why mAP isn't higher (21% vs expected 30-50%):
  1. Traffic sign (19% of GT) contributes 0% AP — no matching COCO class
  2. 62% small boxes (<32px) that YOLO misses
  3. MV propagation drift reduces IoU for frames far from annotation
  4. Only evaluating on train split (no val HEVC videos yet)

5. PHASE D: TRAINING IMPROVEMENTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Loss normalization fix:
  Before: L1 box loss computed in pixel coordinates (0-1280)
    → L1 ≈ 100-200, with lambda=5 → L1 term ≈ 500-1000
    → Total loss ≈ 1000 (dominated by unnormalized box regression)

  After: L1 computed on [0,1]-normalized coordinates
    → L1 ≈ 0.05-0.15, with lambda=5 → L1 term ≈ 0.25-0.75
    → GIoU ≈ 0.5-1.0, focal ≈ 0.5-2.0
    → Total loss ≈ 20-40 (balanced across all three loss terms)

Training configuration:
  - 30 epochs (up from 10)
  - AdamW lr=1e-4, warmup 500 steps, cosine annealing
  - Saves best checkpoint by loss (finetuned_best.pt)
  - All 2000 annotated GOPs, batch_size=1, gradient accumulation=4
  - Mixed precision (AMP), 4 DataLoader workers, pin_memory
  - ~14.5 min/epoch (~7.3 hours total)

Training complete. Loss progression (block-matched features):
  Epoch  1: 30.71    Epoch 10: 29.86    Epoch 20: 29.62    Epoch 30: 29.28
  Best checkpoint: epoch 29, loss 29.26

7. FINAL RESULTS — ALL METHODS COMPARED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Evaluated on 50 annotated GOPs (50 × 16 = 800 frames), BDD100K train split.
All models trained 30 epochs with normalized loss, best checkpoint selected.

  +-----------------------------------+----------+--------------+--------+---------+
  | Method                            | mAP@50   | mAP@[.5:.95] | Decode | Latency |
  +-----------------------------------+----------+--------------+--------+---------+
  | YOLO Full (upper bound)           |  21.01%  |    10.58%    | 100.0% | 233ms   |
  | CoPE-Δ-Det (block-matched MVs)   |  18.90%  |     8.15%    |   6.2% |  96ms   |
  | CoPE-Δ-Det (PyAV H.264 proxy)    |  12.30%  |     5.07%    |   6.2% |  96ms   |
  | Copy-Paste (lower bound)          |  13.66%  |     ---      |   6.2% |  16ms   |
  +-----------------------------------+----------+--------------+--------+---------+

Key results:
  - CoPE (block-matched) captures 90% of YOLO Full accuracy at 6.2% decode cost
  - CoPE vs Copy-Paste: +5.24pp mAP@50 gain from temporal fusion refinement
  - Block-matched MVs >> H.264 proxy MVs: +6.60pp (proxy codec = wrong motion field)
  - 2.4× faster than YOLO Full (96ms vs 233ms per GOP)

Phase 1 → Phase 2 improvement:
